# Screenshot copy

Six captures, 1200 px wide (3× a 400 px panel), trimmed to content, no chrome,
no backdrop, no baked text. Opaque PNG on the panel background `#0B0D12`, so they
drop onto any dark backdrop without a light halo.

They are one story, not six screens: the same task — **find the best wireless
headphones under ₹15,000 for long flights** — asked, run, inspected, answered,
compared, then bought. Keep them in order and the set reads as a session.

| # | File | Px (CSS) | Headline | Sub |
|---|------|----------|----------|-----|
| 1 | `01-home.png` | 400×634 | **Say what you need.** | A browser agent in your side panel. No account, no server, your own key. |
| 2 | `02-running.png` | 400×555 | **It works on the page, not on a server.** | Every click happens in the tab you are watching. Pause or stop it mid-run. |
| 3 | `03-step-open.png` | 400×663 | **Ask why it clicked that.** | Open any step: the reasoning, the result and the screenshot are all there. |
| 4 | `04-answer.png` | 400×609 | **The answer comes first.** | A verdict in the first sentence, and follow-ups stay in the same thread. |
| 5 | `05-rich-answer.png` | 400×1081 | **Comparisons you can actually read.** | Ask for five options and get a table, a chart and the numbers behind them. |
| 6 | `06-approval.png` | 400×700 | **It asks before it spends.** | Checkout, submit, delete — anything that matters stops and waits for you. |

If you only build four slides: **1, 3, 5, 6**. They are the ask, the
transparency, the payoff and the trust.

Alt lines when a slide wants a second sentence:

- 1 — "It reads the page you are on, so it can act on what you see."
- 2 — "Each row is one action: what it did, on which site, at what time."
- 3 — "The reasoning is the model's own; the result is what the page returned."
- 4 — "Twenty steps stay folded above the answer, not spread through it."
- 5 — "Tables sort. Charts come from the values it found, not from a guess."
- 6 — "The approval names the action, the price and the site before anything happens."

## Fitting them to 1280×800

These are portrait crops of a 400 px panel, so no two share a height. Place each
at the height it needs rather than matching them:

- **2, 4, 6** scale up well to about 620–700 px tall on a slide and leave room
  for text on the left.
- **1** works at ~600 px, centred or right, with the greeting carrying it.
- **3** at ~660 px, right side, headline left.
- **5** is 1081 px tall and will not fit beside text. Either give it the full
  slide with the headline above, or crop it from the verdict (the sentence
  "The JBL Tune 770NC is the one for flights…") down to the two stat tiles —
  about 400×760 — and drop the timeline above it.

## Palette

The panel is `#0B0D12`. Accent `#5A9BFF`, success `#22C55E`, warning `#E0A106`.
A backdrop a shade lighter than the panel (`#101319`) gives the screenshot a
soft edge; brighter than that and the panel edge reads as a hard rectangle.
One sans for slide type, 700 headline / 400–500 sub, sentence case, no tracked
capitals.
